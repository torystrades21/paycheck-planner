# Paycheck Planner

A monthly budget app: plan your pay across bills, expenses, savings and debt, then track what you spend.

**Open it:** https://torystrades21.github.io/paycheck-planner/

On a phone, open the link, then choose **Add to Home Screen** (iPhone: the Share button in Safari; Android: the ⋮ menu in Chrome).
It then opens like an app and works with no internet.

Your budget is saved only on your own device. Nothing you type is sent here or anywhere else.
